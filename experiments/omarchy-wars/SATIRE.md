# Enemy-window satire

These are invented archetypes and original jokes, not portraits or allegations
about real commenters. Criticism does not imply hacking or malicious conduct.

- Bloat Patrol: concerns about bundled apps and dependencies.
- Default Police: preference for control over default applications and themes.
- Arch Gatekeeper: the DIY-installation attitude.
- Dotfile Tribunal: arguments over whether a preconfigured setup is a distro.

Research references (read 2026-09-17):
- https://github.com/omacom/omarchy/discussions/1155
  Includes supportive users requesting a smaller installation and selectable apps.
- https://www.reddit.com/r/archlinux/comments/1ofjb50/i_dumped_omarchy_and_went_back_to_a_fresh/
  Discussion of bloat, configuration preferences, and DIY expectations.
- https://www.reddit.com/r/archlinux/comments/1wcfnpy/72h_from_omarchy_to_arch_my_journey_and_what_to/
  Discusses preconfiguration, dotfiles, and newcomer expectations.

The in-game complaints are fictional paraphrases, not quotations.
